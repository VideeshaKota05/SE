**Jira board:** [Pharmacy Lab 2 - Scrum Board](https://aiml-team-se-5c.atlassian.net/jira/software/c/projects/SL/boards/36)  
**Issue range:** `SL-15`–`SL-24`  
**Simulation date:** 2 November 2026  
**Exercise type:** Short Jira status-transition simulation. Both sprint records were closed on the same date; neither planned one-week period elapsed.

## 1. How closely did the estimates match the work?

The seven user stories carry estimates of 3, 5, 3, 5, 8, 3 and 8 points, giving a backlog total of 35 points. These values were entered into Jira's **Story point estimate** field.

At the end of Sprint 1, the 11 planned points were distributed as 3 Done, 5 In Progress and 3 To Do. Sprint 2 closed with 5 of its 16 planned points Done, 8 In Progress and 3 To Do. The results indicate that neither sprint's planned scope was fully completed during the simulation.

It would not be accurate to label a particular story overestimated or underestimated from these status totals alone. No actual effort or elapsed working time was recorded, and there is no documented team Planning Poker result to compare with the estimates.

## 2. Was the priority order appropriate?

The expiry and FEFO stories were placed at the top of the backlog because expiry data is needed before the system can reliably choose the correct batch or prepare the clerk's dispensing list. Secure supplier-order dispatch and operating-hours availability were also assigned High priority because they relate to replenishment reliability and continued pharmacy operations.

Draft purchase-order creation and the supplier's order-view function were assigned Medium priority. The priorities were saved on all seven Jira Stories. This order is a reasonable interpretation of the Lab 1 requirements, although a team refinement discussion could lead to adjustments.

## 3. Did the sprint results match the plan?

Sprint 1 contained US-01, US-02 and US-03, with a planned total of 11 points. On closure:
- US-01 (3 points) was Done.
- US-02 (5 points) was In Progress.
- US-03 (3 points) was To Do.

Sprint 2 contained US-04, US-05 and US-06, with a planned total of 16 points. Its closing states were:
- US-04 (5 points) was Done.
- US-05 (8 points) was In Progress.
- US-06 (3 points) was To Do.

The sprint records were started and closed on 2 November 2026. Consequently, these outcomes represent a quick demonstration of Jira status transitions, rather than a full week of team implementation. The planned scope was entered, but only part of it was marked Done.

## 4. What does the burndown report indicate about capacity?

A genuine Jira Burndown Chart was not captured, and the available Jira connection did not provide the report itself. There is therefore no chart evidence from which to describe the remaining-work line, flat periods, sudden changes or team capacity. The issue-status totals show partial completion, but they cannot replace a burndown report. The actual chart for each sprint must still be opened in Jira Reports and captured for submission.
